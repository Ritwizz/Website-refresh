Altr

Making your work, more you. A modular, personalized desk system for luxury offices.

Furniture / Product Design Team project 2023
Cluttered, messy workspaces create friction that breaks focus and slows down the workday.
The beauty in chaos concept was selected and taken forward.
Five pre-configurations — academia, creative, fiscal, managerial and technical — each a whole desk.
When an employee leaves, the desk is reassigned and re-personalized — a cyclical system rather than a purchase.

ALTR is a modular, personalized desk system for luxury offices. The team was tasked to come up with concepts for office furniture to place in high end, dynamic workspaces, including home offices. My response was ALTR, a fully modular desk system designed specifically as a B2B solution for luxury offices. The core idea is to offer desks that can be hyper-personalized to each user, blending functionality with an unmatched level of customization.

The problem I aimed to address was the common experience of cluttered and messy workspaces. Such environments often lead to confusion and create friction during the workday, which in turn reduces both efficiency and enjoyment. When a desk is disorganized, it's harder to focus, more difficult to find what you need, and the overall flow of work gets interrupted. By solving this problem, ALTR seeks to create a workspace that supports productivity and clarity.

After finalizing the concept, we designed the desk to be compartmentalized into five distinct pre-configurations: academia, creative, fiscal, managerial, and technical. Each compartment functions as an entire desk with specific attachments tailored to different work needs, giving users a strong base to choose from.

As a B2B product, luxury office spaces partner with the manufacturer to assign desks to employees. Users then log on to a website to personalize their chosen configuration. Once completed, desks are delivered and set up. When an employee leaves, the desk can be reassigned, allowing for new customization. The manufacturer evaluates parts for refurbishment or replacement, creating a cyclical, sustainable system that keeps costs manageable while delivering a highly personalized workspace.

The academia desk was designed for academics who engage in extensive research, writing and review, with ample space for books, papers, stationery and personal effects like photos and plants. The creative desk offers storage for tech equipment like drawing tablets and headphones alongside space for figurines and inspiration, with a notice board at the back and a whiteboard module on the left. The fiscal desk provides generous document storage within quick reach, modules for calculators and personal items, and a whiteboard with space for calendars and sticky notes.

The managerial desk features a large noticeboard directly ahead, supported by personalization space to encourage a motivating environment, with calendars and personal effects on the left and extensive document storage on the right. The technical desk includes a medium whiteboard front and center for planning and collaborative problem-solving, pouches for tools, shelves and a pegboard on the left, and a smaller whiteboard and noticeboard on the right.
