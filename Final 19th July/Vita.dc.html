Vita

The grand touring philosophy applied to a scooter for people in their early 20's.

Industrial design Experience design AI experimentation Visualization 2023 · 1 month · Concept
Problem

It's hard to find a scooter that is both practical and exciting to ride.

A scooter that goes the distance

In early 2023, I owned an Aprilia SR 150. I chose excitement over practicality and always wondered why both couldn't co-exist. When I asked around, I realized many people felt the same. That was the motivation behind this project.

The process

A lot of sketching and vizcom iterations, followed by refinement sketches and full development in Blender.
